# Governance

Babel Protocol uses Babel Advancement Proposals, or BAPs, for protocol changes.

## Principles

- Live conversation is primary.
- Private by default.
- Permission before preservation.
- AI cannot publish.
- Original language remains authoritative.
- Repair is normal.
- Cultural context is first-class.
- Blockchain is optional and not part of v0.1.
- Cloudflare is infrastructure, not authority.
- Technology remains replaceable.

## Maintainer Role

Maintainers steward implementation quality, review protocol changes, protect privacy and consent boundaries, and keep the project aligned with its mission.

Maintainers cannot override participant consent in a Babel room.

## BAP Process

Use `specifications/BAP-TEMPLATE.md`. Accepted BAPs must describe privacy impact, consent impact, accessibility impact, failure modes, portability, and tests.
