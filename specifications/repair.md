# Repair

Participants may mark a message, translation, or interpretation with:

```text
I was misunderstood
Translation is inaccurate
Important context is missing
This wording is harmful
I disagree with this interpretation
I need clarification
Please preserve my original wording
```

Repair states:

```text
open
acknowledged
clarifying
resolved
unresolved
withdrawn
```

Corrections do not erase originals. Downstream artifact approvals become invalid when the exact revision changes.
