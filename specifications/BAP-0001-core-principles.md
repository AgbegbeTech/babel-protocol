# BAP-0001: Core Principles

## Status

Accepted for Babel Protocol v0.1.

## Principles

Live conversation is primary. A private conversation that produces no artifact is successful.

Private by default. Conversation content does not automatically become public content, Commons entries, search results, public transcripts, AI training data, public embeddings, analytics containing message content, or marketing material.

Permission before preservation. Nothing derived from a conversation enters The Commons until every required participant approves the exact artifact revision.

AI cannot publish. AI may assist live communication, but it cannot approve, publish, infer consent, impersonate participants, declare consensus, alter approval, silently expose private content, or become final authority on meaning.

Original language remains authoritative. Translations are interpretations and never overwrite original messages.

Repair is normal. Misunderstanding is expected and must be visible, non-punitive, and repairable.

Cultural context is first-class. Meaning may include regional context, historical context, tone, idiom, uncertainty, minority interpretation, and power imbalance.

Blockchain is optional. v0.1 does not implement blockchain, tokens, wallets, gas, smart contracts, or cryptocurrency.

Cloudflare is infrastructure, not authority. Technology remains replaceable.
