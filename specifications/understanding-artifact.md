# Understanding Artifact

Understanding Artifacts are downstream. They never appear automatically.

Lifecycle:

```text
draft
proposed
under_review
approved
published
withdrawn
```

Artifacts may include a title, originating question, shared summary, cultural contexts, linguistic ambiguities, areas of agreement, unresolved disagreements, minority perspectives, evidence references, approved translations, required approvers, revision history, AI disclosure, and publication scope.

Artifacts do not expose transcripts or private message IDs.
