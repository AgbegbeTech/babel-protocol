# BAP-0002: Dialogue, Freedom, and Difference

## Status

Accepted for Babel Protocol v0.1.

## Summary

Babel rooms create understanding through participation rather than transmission. This BAP records design commitments inspired in part by bell hooks' work on dialogue, voice, engaged pedagogy, community, domination, care, love, critical consciousness, and education as a practice of freedom.

Babel is independent and does not claim endorsement by bell hooks, her estate, or her publishers.

## Commitments

Every participant must be able to speak, question, correct, clarify, disagree, revise, add cultural context, challenge a translation, challenge an AI interpretation, refuse preservation, refuse publication, and leave the conversation.

Voice is not the same as visibility. Babel distinguishes being permitted to speak from being accurately understood, influencing shared interpretation, controlling attribution, approving publication, and participating in resulting action.

Difference must survive translation. Original messages remain available beside translations. Tone, emotion, uncertainty, cultural specificity, and disagreement must not be flattened for cleaner summaries.

Understanding is not forced agreement. Outcomes may include agreement, partial agreement, clearer disagreement, unresolved questions, a need for more context, or no public artifact.

Care is operational: informed consent, accessibility, honest uncertainty, non-extractive data practices, protection from humiliation, participant control, fair attribution, repair, respect for boundaries, and refusal to optimize for outrage.
