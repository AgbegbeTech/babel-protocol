# Roadmap

## v0.1

- Live one-to-one multilingual conversation room
- Original-first messages and translation child events
- Mock translation and mock AI facilitation
- Clarification, cultural context, and repair
- Revision-specific artifact consent
- Approved Commons publication without transcript leakage
- Collaboration project creation from approved artifact
- Cloudflare hosted-edge reference architecture
- Local fallback without Cloudflare

## Future Work

- Group rooms
- passkeys and WebAuthn
- hardware-backed device keys
- recovery delegates
- end-to-end encryption and MLS research
- provider integrations for translation
- provider integrations for AI facilitation
- federation adapters
- voice session association with room identity and consent rules
- institutional archive adapters

Out of scope for v0.1: blockchain, tokens, smart contracts, DAO governance, public federation, native mobile apps, live voice, video, payment processing, Kubernetes, automated moderation decisions, and public reputation scores.
