# Code of Conduct

Babel Protocol is built for communication across difference. Participation in this project requires care, honesty, patience, and respect for people whose language, cultural context, safety needs, or technical access differ from your own.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, humiliation, threats, doxxing, intentional misrepresentation, coercive pressure for publication, and attempts to extract private conversation content.

Maintainers may remove content, close issues, block accounts, or take other proportionate steps to protect contributors and participants.
