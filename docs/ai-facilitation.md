# AI Facilitation

AI facilitation is off by default except in the development demo, where the mock provider is explicitly visible.

AI may:

- clarify a phrase
- identify possible ambiguity
- suggest a neutral restatement
- identify apparent agreement as a possibility
- identify unresolved disagreement
- ask whether translation reflects the speaker's intent
- suggest a clarification question

AI may not:

- approve
- publish
- sign
- infer consent
- impersonate a participant
- declare consensus
- become final authority on meaning

Every AI response must disclose that it was generated by AI, what context it considered, uncertainty, missing context, and that participants may revise or reject it.
